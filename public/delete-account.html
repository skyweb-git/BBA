Request Account & Data Deletion

We respect your privacy and right to be forgotten. Learn how to immediately delete your Artube account and personal data.

Option 1: Delete Directly in the Artube App (Instant)

If you have the Artube app installed on your Android device, you can delete your account and data immediately:

  1. 1 Open the Artube app on your device.
  2. 2 Tap on your Profile tab in the bottom navigation.
  3. 3 Open Settings → select Account Settings.
  4. 4 Scroll to the bottom and select Delete Account.
  5. 5 Read the confirmation warning and confirm deletion. Your account session will terminate immediately and your data will be permanently removed.

Option 2: Web / Email Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the application or can no longer access your device, submit a web request to our privacy team:

Email: support@artube.app

Subject: Account and Data Deletion Request - Artube

Required Details: Please send the email from the email address registered with your Artube account and include your Username. Our team will verify and delete your account within 7 business days.

Send Deletion Request Email

What Data Is Deleted?

  • Profile Data: Your name, username, biography, profile avatar, banner cover, and social links.
  • User Content: All uploaded artwork images, videos, descriptions, tags, and course lessons.
  • Community Interactions: Comments, likes, saves, and following connections.
  • Credentials & Security Tokens: Password hashes, OAuth tokens, and push notification device tokens.

Retention Note: We do not retain personal data following deletion. Certain transaction records or security logs may be retained only for the minimum period legally required for anti-fraud, legal dispute defense, or tax compliance.
