1. Who We Are & Scope

Welcome to Artube (“the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), a mobile community and streaming platform crafted for visual artists, creators, and learners. Artube is owned and operated by ArtArtist, with software engineering and infrastructure maintained by the SkyWeb technology team.

This Privacy Policy specifically governs the Android application distributed under the package name com.artube.app and the web services hosted under https://artube.artartist.in. It explains transparently how we collect, store, utilize, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you install, create an account with, or interact with Artube.

Transparency Promise: We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data, audio recordings, or artistic content to data brokers or third-party advertising networks.

2. Information We Collect

To deliver video streaming, live audio radio, artwork sharing, and educational courses, Artube collects only the information necessary to fulfill these core services. We collect the following categories of data:

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Account Registration Details: When you register an account, we collect your name, username, email address, and encrypted authentication credentials. If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In, we collect your verified Google name, email address, Google ID, and profile photo provided by Google OAuth.
  • Artist Profile Details: Information you choose to add to your public creator profile, such as biography, portfolio links, art categories/skills, profile avatar, and header cover image.
  • User-Generated Content (UGC): Artwork photos, video demonstrations, audio streams, course lesson notes, comments, likes, playlist saves, and community discussions that you post or share.
  • Creator & Payout Settings (Optional): If you participate in Creator monetization, course offerings, or tip features, you may optionally provide payout preferences (such as PayPal email or bank details). Payout details are strictly stored for processing transactions and are never shared for advertising.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • App Interactions & Usage: Videos watched, artwork views, creators followed, live radio rooms tuned into, search queries, and interaction timestamps.
  • Device & Diagnostic Information: Device model, operating system version, app version (e.g., 1.0.1), preferred language, crash logs, and network connection status (Wi-Fi/Cellular).
  • Push Notification Tokens: A unique device push token (via Expo Notifications / Firebase Cloud Messaging) to deliver alerts regarding new uploads, comments, follower updates, and live broadcast events.

3. Sensitive Permissions & Hardware Access

In accordance with Google Play's Sensitive User and Device Data Policy, Artube requests only permissions essential for user-facing features, with explicit runtime prompts and clear in-app disclosures:

Hardware Permissions Summary:
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used exclusively during user-initiated live radio sessions (“Artwave Live”) and speech recognition (“Voice Search”). Artube never records audio in the background or when the app is inactive.
  • Photos & Storage (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / Gallery): Used only when you select an image or video file to publish an artwork post, upload an educational lesson, or update your profile avatar. We do not scan or upload your private photo gallery.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to notify you of interactions, live events from artists you follow, and system announcements. You can turn notifications on or off at any time in App Settings or Android System Settings.
  • Wake Lock (WAKE_LOCK): Ensures smooth, stutter-free playback of Artwave Live audio and video streams without display interruptions.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  1. Core App Functionality: Providing account authentication, user profiles, video player feeds, course catalogs, and community comments.
  2. Artwave Live & Audio Broadcasts: Powering real-time WebRTC audio streams so artists can host radio talks and listeners can tune in.
  3. Voice-Powered Search: Translating spoken queries into text inside the search bar so you can find tutorials and creators without typing.
  4. Safety & Community Integrity: Enforcing our Terms of Service, filtering prohibited or sexually exploitative content, preventing automated bots, and enabling user blocking and reporting tools.
  5. Customer Support & Communication: Responding to user inquiries, policy clarification requests, bug reports, and sending critical security notices.

6. Third-Party Services & SDK Disclosures

Artube integrates selected industry-standard software development kits (SDKs) and cloud services to ensure reliable application performance. These providers process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Google Play Services & Google Sign-In: Used to provide seamless, secure OAuth authentication. Refer to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Expo & Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Used to deliver push notifications and handle runtime application updates. Refer to Expo's Privacy Policy.
  • WebRTC: Facilitates low-latency peer-to-peer audio transmission for Artwave Live radio broadcasts. Audio frames are streamed in real time and are not permanently archived without creator consent.
  • Cloud Infrastructure & Databases (MongoDB): Secured backend infrastructure hosted with encrypted storage to maintain account data and user feeds.

7. Data Retention & Security

We employ technical, administrative, and physical safeguards designed to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS): All communications between the Artube mobile application and our backend server (https://artube.artartist.in) are encrypted using modern SSL/TLS protocols.
  • Password Protection: If you register with an email and password, your password is encrypted using strong salted hashing algorithms (bcrypt) and is never visible in plaintext.
  • Retention Policy: We retain account data for as long as your account remains active. When you delete your account, your profile, uploads, comments, and tokens are permanently removed from active databases, subject only to minimal retention required by law or security logging.

8. User Rights & In-App Privacy Controls

Artube provides users with complete autonomy over their privacy settings within the app:

  • Account Privacy Mode: You can toggle between a public profile or a private profile from App Settings.
  • Account Blocking: You can block any artist or user at any time. Blocked accounts cannot view your feed, follow you, or send you notifications.
  • Restricted Keyword Filtering: You can set custom word filters to automatically screen out unwanted terms in comments.
  • Mature Content (18+) Filter: Sensitive artistic content is restricted by default and can only be enabled by verified adult accounts.
  • Revoking Permissions: You can revoke Microphone, Gallery, or Notification permissions at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Artube → Permissions.

9. Account & Data Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, Artube provides two straightforward methods for you to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data:

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Instant)

  1. Open the Artube application on your mobile device.
  2. Navigate to the Profile / Settings tab.
  3. Tap on Account Settings → scroll to Delete Account.
  4. Confirm your deletion request. Your account, profile, uploads, and data will be immediately scheduled for permanent purging.

Method 2: Web / Email Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot log in, you can request account deletion by emailing our support team:

  • Email: support@artube.app
  • Subject Line: Account Deletion Request - Artube
  • Content: Include your registered email address and username. We will process your deletion request within 7 business days and confirm once completed.
  • Web Page: You can also visit our dedicated Account & Data Deletion Portal.

10. Children’s Privacy (Under 13)

Artube is a general audience creative platform intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under 13 years of age.

If we become aware that an account has been registered by a child under the age of 13 without verifiable parental consent, we will promptly delete that account and all associated information from our servers. If you believe a child under 13 has registered with Artube, please contact us immediately at support@artube.app.

11. International Transfers & Regional Rights

Your information may be stored and processed on secure cloud servers located outside your jurisdiction. We take all appropriate security measures to ensure that your data is treated securely and in accordance with this Privacy Policy regardless of location.

Depending on your region (e.g., European Economic Area / GDPR, California / CCPA), you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections, obtain an export of your information, or object to specific processing activities. To exercise any of these statutory rights, contact support@artube.app.
